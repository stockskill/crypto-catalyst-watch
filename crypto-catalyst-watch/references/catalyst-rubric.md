# Catalyst rubric

## Materiality test

Call an update material only when it plausibly changes at least one of these:

- global liquidity, regulatory access, ETF demand, or institutional risk appetite;
- circulation, unlock/emission, treasury or ecosystem supply pressure;
- protocol capability, reliability, security, staking, fee/revenue, or user activity;
- leverage, derivatives positioning, or forced-liquidation risk.

Routine marketing posts, price commentary, unlabelled-wallet rumours, and recycled headlines are not material.

## Required synthesis

Before assigning BUY or SELL, answer:

1. What was the prior expectation?
2. What verified fact changed it?
3. Which second data layer confirms or contradicts it?
4. Which event or level would invalidate the interpretation?

If any answer is missing, use NEUTRAL / WATCH.

## Deep Scan checklist

- Price snapshot and relative strength: BTC versus ETH versus SOL
- Macro event calendar, yields, dollar, and risk appetite when relevant
- ETF flows and issuer/filing changes
- Exchange flow and attributed/unattributed large-wallet transactions
- Funding, OI, basis, liquidations, and options only with visible current data
- Heatmap zones above and below spot, with provider and time
- Network status, upgrade/security/governance event, and supply/unlock risk
- One clear next decisive event and one clear invalidation condition
