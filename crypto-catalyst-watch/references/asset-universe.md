# Asset universe and event map

## Core assets

| Asset | Focus | High-value catalysts | Primary-source priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| BTC | Macro liquidity and institutional access | central-bank decisions, inflation/jobs data, spot-ETF flow, exchange or custody disruption, material holder movement, regulation | central banks, SEC/regulators, ETF filings/issuers, exchange status, Bitcoin Core/security disclosures |
| ETH | Smart-contract platform and ETF demand | ETF flow, protocol/client upgrade, staking/validator conditions, L2 or fee/revenue shift, major security incident, regulation | Ethereum Foundation/client teams, official EIPs/releases, SEC/regulators, ETF issuers, exchange status |
| SOL | High-beta L1 and application activity | validator/client release, uptime/performance issue, institutional product flow, major application or DeFi event, token supply event, security incident | Solana Foundation/client teams, validator/status data, regulators, fund issuers, exchange status |

## Mandatory current-data layers

1. Spot price and timestamp
2. Macro calendar and risk regime
3. ETF/institutional flow where products exist
4. Exchange flow and labelled whale activity
5. Funding, open interest, basis, liquidations, and options where verifiable
6. Liquidation heatmap with source and retrieval time
7. Network status, protocol/security events, and token-supply changes

## Source hierarchy

1. Official government, regulator, central-bank, protocol, client, issuer, exchange, and on-chain explorer sources
2. Established market-data providers with transparent methodology
3. Reputable reporting that attributes data to a named provider
4. Community/social posts only as leads; never as proof
